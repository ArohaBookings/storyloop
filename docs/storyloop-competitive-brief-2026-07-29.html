Competitive and growth brief · 29 July 2026

Win the educator workflow before trying to replace the centre system.

StoryLoop’s credible wedge is not “Storypark with AI.” It is the shortest trustworthy path from a real moment to educator-reviewed documentation, continuity, planning and export—while giving educators more time with children.

Executive view

Problem

Documentation is consuming child-facing and unpaid time.

Australian research reported educators spending under 30% of the day in focused interaction, with more than three quarters averaging nine unpaid hours weekly. NZ sector voices likewise describe compliance and paperwork load.

Product answer

Capture lightly; decide deliberately.

Today Loop captures one real moment, surfaces unfinished next steps, and lets the educator choose story, planning or archive. It avoids building a second compulsory daily sheet.

Commercial answer

Individual adoption first, centre visibility second.

Free demonstrates value; Educator removes limits; Pro deepens family continuity; Centre tiers sell consistency, planning and leadership visibility without educator ranking.

What educators and requirements are signalling

SignalEvidenceImplicationStoryLoop response
Too little focused interaction time University of Sydney research: under 30% of the day in focused interaction; substantial unpaid workload. New features must reduce steps rather than create reporting obligations. Today Loop, Backlog Rescue, voice/text capture and exports.
Assessment is a cycle, not a story quota Te Whāriki guidance uses noticing, recognising, responding, recording and revisiting, with formal and informal assessment and whānau partnership. Do not imply every observation becomes a polished learning story. Explicit story/planning/archive decision and open follow-up prompts.
Images require stronger governance ACECQA child-safety changes and the National Model Code strengthen expectations around authorised devices and image policies. Photo-first engagement loops create avoidable privacy and policy friction. Text-first daily capture; photo never required; privacy/evidence checks.
Incumbent frustration centres on reliability Directional App Store and Play reviews mention long uploads, freezes, login trouble and lost work. These are anecdotes, not a representative survey. Reliability, saved drafts and clear recovery matter more than feature count. Editable history, narrow requests, clear errors, build-safe public pages, end-to-end QA.

Recommended tier architecture

Free

Prove the habit

3 stories and 10 Today Loop moments monthly. Core quality/privacy checks, editable history and curriculum links. No credit card.

Educator

Remove the backlog ceiling

Unlimited stories and moments, Quill allowance, voice, Coach, family pack, export, threads, Backlog Rescue and personal voice.

Educator Pro

Deepen continuity

Unlimited Quill, Family Reply Loop, translation, detailed scoring, child continuity and whānau voice.

Centre Starter

Make practice visible

Calibration, centre voice, planning board, Documentation Radar, admin signals and priority support.

Centre Growth

Prove rollout value

ROI, multi-room analytics, advanced export, rollout health, onboarding and roadmap access.

90-day go-to-market guidance

  1. Lead with the daily problem: “Stay present now; decide what deserves documentation later.”
  2. Show trust, not magic: exact quotes, evidence anchors, assumptions and educator review on the product page and demo.
  3. Acquire individuals organically: evidence-led AU/NZ guides, practical templates, ECE educator group feedback and the no-signup live demo.
  4. Convert centres from proof: after repeat individual use, sell planning visibility, centre voice, calibration and ROI—not surveillance.
  5. Instrument every claim: growth overview, recent signups, plan mix, source attribution, quality pass rate, Today Loop decisions and review queue through admin and the read-only MCP.
